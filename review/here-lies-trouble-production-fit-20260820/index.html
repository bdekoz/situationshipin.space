Here Lies Trouble — production fit assessment

May 2026 network needs vs the Final Draft 13 enrichment of the canonical source transcript. v1-deepseek text pass; verdicts cite each house's green (wants) and red (rejects) lists.

Onyx Collective — STRONG

Matched categories: underrepresented voices; voice-driven character drama; soaps. Direct brand fit: underrepresented voices, character-driven one-hour drama, soap/romance/thriller appetite; the 'reclaim your own story' premise is squarely in Onyx territory.

Brand: Content brand for creators of color and underrepresented voices.

FX — MODERATE

Matched categories: writer/voice driven; distinctive POV; Gen Z demo. FX wants different yet commercial voices and younger demos; the unfictionalized dinner-conversation format is distinctive, though not in a named bucket.

Brand: Elevated adult fare with a distinctive POV.

HBO — MODERATE

Matched categories: interesting voices. HBO's 'interesting voices' bucket fits; adult ongoing drama fits their shelf, but the subject is not a world-building engine. Conflicts: no clear engine.

Brand: Premium and elevated content, through and through.

Hulu — MODERATE

Matched categories: female-driven; ongoing with a meta engine. The 'reclaim your story' meta-premise could carry a thriller/soap engine; female-led conversation material; no horror/period conflicts.

Brand: Both reflects and pierces popular culture.

Netflix — MODERATE

Matched categories: voice-driven character dramas (BEEF, MAID); coming-of-age/Gen Z. Strong voice-driven bucket fit, but the subject is an inside-theater/industry conversation, which lands on Netflix's Inside Hollywood/Showbiz no-fly list. Conflicts: Inside Hollywood/Showbiz no-fly.

Brand: Reaching the widest audience by means of premium, commercial and global fare.

Peacock — MODERATE

Matched categories: water cooler; soapy. Water-cooler/soapy fit is plausible, but the priority is African American audience drama, which this subject does not center. Conflicts: African American audience priority.

Brand: Elevated content that's entertaining or commercial.

Starz — WEAK-MODERATE

Matched categories: premium female focus; ensemble. Female-focused premium and ensemble conversation partially fit, but the subject lacks the thriller/romance engine Starz programs. Conflicts: no romance or crime engine.

Brand: Premium content with a female focus.

ABC — WEAK

ABC wants soap-cedural procedurals with closed arcs; the subject is straight serialized conversation material. Conflicts: straight serialized soaps no-fly.

Brand: Elevated procedurals with iconic characters and strong story engines.

Amazon — WEAK

Amazon wants big, loud, commercial global tentpoles/IP; the subject is intimate and voice-driven rather than event TV. Conflicts: not tentpole/IP.

Brand: Big tentpoles with global appeal.

Apple — WEAK

Apple wants aspirational, star-attractable, escapist popcorn with a big hook; a meta-theater dinner conversation does not fit the mandate. Conflicts: no big hook.

Brand: Sophisticated popcorn with a big hook.

CBS — WEAK

CBS wants franchise procedurals and rejects straight serialization. Conflicts: straight serialization no-fly.

Brand: Big, commercial, relatable hits with mass appeal and zeitgeist relevance.

FOX — WEAK

Matched categories: signature character (partial). Fox wants signature-character franchises and must-returning series; the subject has no procedural engine. Conflicts: must be returning with engine.

Brand: Signature characters at the center of launchable franchises.

HBO Max — WEAK

HBO Max wants action/procedurals/feel-good soap; the subject is not in-camera action or a procedural.

Brand: More popcorn in tone than HBO proper but with a premium lens.

MGM+ — WEAK

MGM+ wants smart comfort food, action-adventure romance, and palace intrigue; no bucket matches.

Brand: Legacy studio with rich, traditional Hollywood fare.

NBC — WEAK

NBC wants workplace/star-vehicle procedurals and mystery-box ideas; the subject does not fit.

Brand: Traditional buying cycle with open-minded commercial taste.

Paramount+ — WEAK

Matched categories: female-forward soap (partial). Female-forward soap is the only partial match; Paramount wants broad premium commercial with an engine and is focused on space/video-game IP. Conflicts: no commercial engine.

Brand: Premium, broad, commercial appeal post-Skydance merger.

Disney+ — CONFLICT

Disney+ wants four-quad family co-viewing with life-and-death stakes and rejects straight character drama; the adult dinner conversation conflicts with the brand. Conflicts: straight character drama no-fly; anything dark; social stakes.

Brand: Heart, humor and wish fulfillment while entertaining a wide audience.

Paramount+ with Showtime — N/A

Showtime is folded into the studio and not buying new content.

Brand: Studio executives covering the Showtime slate only.
