Initial root-cause findings

V5's geodesic weights preserve connected alpha, but cannot reconstruct an occluded rear leg or the changing silhouette of a paw from a single source pose. Connected pixels did not establish anatomical quality. Its two-segment limb targets also simplify the equine fetlock and canine/feline hock. Head foreshortening changes width without supplying the far eye, muzzle, ear or neck surfaces revealed by a real turn. The bird's former landing moves feet between points without a coordinated pelvis/knee/ankle and body impulse.

The new source-pose experiment addresses these representation limits rather than treating a larger pixel difference as evidence of better animation. Actual source coverage, matte quality and temporal continuity still require inspection; the existing reference cannot supply unseen views automatically.
